# DR-009 — `--texto-sutil` fails on a tinted stream line, in both tints and both themes

Status: drafted — NOT sent
Raised: 2026-08-10, Phase 4 Step 4 — manifest entry **41 (Logs)**, during the DR-007 rework (D-094)
Blocks: nothing. Entry 41 is built and shipped; the four pairs are excluded by selector with their
measured ratios pinned as floors.

---

## How it was found, because that is part of the finding

DR-007's answer closed the two gaps it was asked about, so `logs.spec.js` **re-enabled the
`color-contrast` rule** on the stream pass — it had been disabled only while those pairs were below
AA. Re-enabling it immediately surfaced a third composition that the blanket exemption had been
covering all along, unmeasured.

That is the whole argument for un-pinning an exclusion the moment its request is answered: the
exclusion was hiding more than the thing it was written for.

## What fails

`template-console-stream.md` §1 specifies both halves independently, and each is fine on its own:

- an unselected line's **structure** — the timestamp and the source — is `--texto-sutil`;
- an **ERROR** or **WARN** line takes its tint as the line's **background**, at 11 % / 19 %.

Composed, that is `--texto-sutil` on a tint over `--fondo-ventana`:

| Composition | Light | Dark |
|---|---|---|
| `--texto-sutil` on `--tinte-peligro` over `--fondo-ventana` | **3.88:1** | **4.18:1** |
| `--texto-sutil` on `--tinte-aviso` over `--fondo-ventana` | **3.94:1** | **3.73:1** |

All four are below the 4.5:1 threshold. Recomputed independently in Python from the token hexes in
`tokens/colors.css` and `tokens/klytos-admin.css`, compositing each `rgba()` tint before taking the
ratio; axe reports 3.93 for the light/`aviso` case and the arithmetic agrees.

For contrast with what passes: `--texto-sutil` on `--fondo-ventana` alone is **4.53 / 5.44**, and it
is in the audit as a PASS. The surface is the difference.

## Why this is the delivery's and not the build's

Both halves are specified. §1 names `--texto-sutil` for structure and names the tint as the line
background for ERROR and WARN; the build chose neither. Substituting a colour here would be exactly
the Phase 4 rule 2 violation DR-005 exists to avoid, so it is registered rather than fixed — the same
treatment as DR-005's four pairs.

**And the delivery already states the rule this breaks.** The composed-pairs table DR-007 added to
`color-contrast-audit.md` closes with:

> a token that passes on a surface is not thereby passing on a tint over that surface. Any new
> composition — a tint on a row state, a badge on a selected row — is measured before it is
> specified.

That table measures `--texto-sutil` on `--fila-seleccion` over `--fondo-ventana` (3.89 / 3.39, marked
FAIL and withdrawn) and `--texto-sutil` on `--fondo-ventana` (4.53 / 5.44, PASS). It never measures
`--texto-sutil` on a **semantic tint** over that surface — which is what every ERROR and every WARN
line in the product renders.

## What is NOT affected, and it narrows the request

**Selected** tinted lines are fine. DR-007's answer makes every role inside a selected line
`--texto-primario`, which measures **12.67 / 11.74** on `--tinte-peligro` and **12.86 / 10.49** on
`--tinte-aviso`. So this is the **unselected** state only, and the exclusion in `logs.spec.js` is
scoped with `:not([aria-pressed="true"])` so the selected state stays fully checked.

## What is asked of Design

One decision, and there are at least three shapes it could take — the choice is Design's:

1. **A `--sobre-tinte-sutil` pair**, the way `--sobre-tinte-*` already solves this for the level
   labels: a subtler token per tint that still clears 4.5:1 over it.
2. **Structure takes `--texto-secundario` on tinted lines only** — but note `--texto-secundario` on
   `--tinte-peligro` over `--fondo-ventana` needs measuring before it is chosen; it is not obviously
   better and DR-005 gap 2 already found that token failing on the plain surface.
3. **Structure takes `--texto-primario` on tinted lines**, which is what the selected state already
   does and is known to pass at 10.49:1 or better — at the cost of the scanning contrast between
   content and structure that §1 is going for.
