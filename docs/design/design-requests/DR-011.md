# DR-011 — A chat-only way into Klytos: is the conversation ever the whole screen?

Status: drafted — NOT sent
Raised: 2026-08-11, Phase 4 Step 4 — manifest entry **12 (AI chat)**, stage 6 slice 3 (D-108)
Blocks: **nothing.** Entry 12 is built, driven and shipped to the delivery as it stands, with the
shell. This asks whether a second, shell-less mode should exist beside it.

---

## How it was raised, because that matters here

Not by a gap in the delivery. It was raised by **the product owner, in the conversation**, while the
build was being scoped:

> «Yo quiero que se pueda entrar en Klytos y solo ver un chat al que se le pida cualquier cosa.»
> *("I want it to be possible to enter Klytos and see only a chat you can ask anything of.")*

That is a product intent the delivery does not describe, and Phase 4 rule 2 says a build never
invents one. So entry 12 was built exactly as specified, and the intent is registered here rather
than acted on.

## What the delivery specifies today, and why it is not that

`SPEC/manifest.md` §12 calls the screen **`ai.php` · conversation (full screen) · H1 "Klytos AI"**,
and "full screen" turns out to mean *the conversation occupies the whole of `main`* — not *the shell
is absent*:

| Artifact | What it says |
|---|---|
| `SPEC/navigation.md` §3 | **AI chat** is an ordinary sidebar item in the *Intelligence* group, glyph `ks-auto_awesome`, no count |
| `template-conversation.md` §4 | At 900–1199 "Sidebar → icon rail"; below 900 "Sidebar → drawer" — describing the shell's behaviour **on this screen** |
| `template-conversation.md` §5 | "Full-screen chat: the transcript is inside `<main>`" — `main` is a shell landmark |
| `accessibility.md` §4.1 | Every authenticated screen carries `banner`, `navigation`, `main`, `contentinfo`. "Auth pages have `main` only — no shell" is stated as the exception, and this is not an auth page |

Four artifacts agree, so the screen was built with the shell. Nothing here is ambiguous — which is
exactly why the owner's intent needs a decision from Design rather than a reading from the build.

**Worth knowing:** what shipped before this rewrite was already a shell-less chat, achieved with five
`display: none !important` rules over the redesign's own sidebar, toolbar and status bar, plus a
`?panel=` router that re-implemented Dashboard, Settings, Users and Profile *inside* the chat. That
is what the redesign replaced. The intent behind it, though, was never recorded anywhere — so this
request also exists to stop it being re-derived from scratch by the next person who reads that code
in history.

## What we are asking Design to decide

1. **Is there a chat-only mode at all?** A conversation with no sidebar, no toolbar and no status
   bar — a way to open Klytos and simply talk to it.
2. **If yes, what is it?** Three shapes, and they are very different products:
   - a **mode toggle** on entry 12 (a control that hides the shell, remembered per user);
   - a **separate entry point** (its own URL, its own row in `SPEC/manifest.md`) that is a
     conversation and nothing else;
   - a **landing choice** — the screen a person arrives at after signing in.
3. **How does someone leave it?** A screen with no navigation needs a specified way back, and
   §4.1's landmark set has to be answered for whatever this is: a page with `main` alone is
   currently reserved to the auth screens.
4. **What is its `<h1>`, and does it keep "Klytos AI"?**
5. **What happens to the copilot dock?** The dock is the delivery's own answer to "talk to Klytos
   from wherever you are"; a chat-only mode is the opposite move, and whether they coexist is a
   product decision, not a layout one.

## What we are NOT asking

Nothing about the conversation itself. §1's anatomy, §2's states, §3's dock modes, §4's
breakpoints and §5's accessibility are all built or deferred with recorded reasons
(`docs/roadmap.md` §0c), and none of them changes with the answer. This is only about the frame.

> **only a chat**, with nothing else on the screen, and ask it for anything.

>
> 1. Which shape is it — a mode toggle on entry 12, a separate entry point with its own manifest
