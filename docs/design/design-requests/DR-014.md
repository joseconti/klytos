# DR-014 — *Settlement lag* is a stat with no clock behind it

**Status:** drafted 2026-08-11 · **NOT SENT** — sending is the user's call
**Raised by:** D-112 (Phase 4 Step 4, stage 7 slice 3 — manifest entry 18)
**Blocks:** nothing. Entry 18 is built with the four stats the product can measure.

## What is missing

`SPEC/manifest.md` §18 gives the screen five stat cards:

> - Stats: Revenue (30d) · Paid requests · Unique agents · Avg. price · Settlement lag

Four of them are real and are built. **The fifth cannot be computed.**

A Klytos x402 transaction is written once, by `TransactionLog::log()`, with
exactly one timestamp:

- `created_at` — set to `gmdate()` at the moment the record is written;
- `facilitator_ok` — a **boolean**, with no time beside it;
- `tx_hash` — a string, frequently empty, with no time beside it either.

**Nothing anywhere records when settlement happened.** A lag is the distance
between two instants, and the product holds one. There is no second write to the
record after the facilitator answers, no settlement collection, and no field on
any other record that could supply the missing end of the interval.

This is not a formatting question or a missing label: it is a **capability**.
Measuring settlement lag means the gate recording a second timestamp when the
facilitator confirms, a migration for records already written (this product is
released and has installs in the field with stored transactions), and a decision
about what the figure means for a transaction that never settled at all.

## What the build did

Four cards — **Revenue (30 days) · Paid requests · Unique agents · Avg. price** —
which is inside `template-overview-stats.md` §1's floor of 3–5, and every one of
them is a measured fact:

- Revenue and Paid requests come straight from `Stats::getSummary()['month']`.
- Unique agents is the count of distinct agents in the same 30-day window,
  taken from `Stats::getTopBots()` so the grouping rule is the one the *Agents by
  spend* card already uses rather than a second, subtly different one.
- Avg. price is a mean of measured values, and it renders `—` rather than `0`
  when there are no requests, because §2 is explicit that those are different
  claims.

§18's delta is honoured throughout: all money is numeric, right-aligned, with
the currency **as text**.
