# DR-010 — The Taxonomies screen has no selection state, so its own nav item cannot reach it

Status: drafted — NOT sent
Raised: 2026-08-10, Phase 4 Step 4 — manifest entry **32 (Taxonomies)**, stage 5 batch B (D-102)
Blocks: the screen's H1 and its nav destination. It does NOT block the add-term form, which is
built; the screen is reachable and fully usable today from entry 19's per-taxonomy links.

---

## How it was found, because that is part of the finding

Entry 32 is the **first consumer** of the `taxonomies` row in `SPEC/navigation.md`, which stage 2
built into the sidebar of every admin page. Being the first consumer of an existing extension point
is where this project keeps finding defects — entry 28 found a contrast failure the same way, as
the first listener on `admin.statusbar_degraded`.

Following that nav item is what surfaced this: it goes to Content model, and always has.

## What contradicts what

Two recorded artifacts of the delivery describe the same screen and cannot both be built:

| Artifact | What it says |
|---|---|
| `SPEC/manifest.md` §32 | ONE screen, `taxonomies.php`, **H1 "Taxonomies"**, carrying a terms table and an add-term form |
| `SPEC/navigation.md` §3 | A nav item **Taxonomies** → that screen, at a bare URL with no parameters |

And the product:

> A taxonomy is stored **inside a post type's own record** (`PostTypeManager::addTaxonomy()`), and
> its terms live in a per-pair collection, `terms-{postType}-{taxonomy}`. Two post types may each
> hold a taxonomy of the same id. There is no global list of taxonomies and no such thing as "the"
> taxonomy.

So the screen cannot render without knowing **which** taxonomy of **which** post type, and
`installer/admin/taxonomy.php` has always redirected to `post-types.php` when either is missing.

**The consequence, on every install since stage 2:** the primary-navigation item named "Taxonomies"
has never once reached the screen it names. It bounces to Content model, silently — no error, no
message, nothing that looks like a defect to whoever clicks it.

## What we are NOT doing, and why it is being asked rather than decided

Three build-side resolutions were available and all three are the assistant inventing product
design, which Phase 4 rule 3 forbids:

1. **Point the nav item at `post-types.php`.** Duplicates entry 19's own nav item and quietly
   deletes a specified screen from the navigation.
2. **Render an invented "choose a taxonomy" state.** No file in the delivery specifies it — not its
   layout, not its empty state, not what it says when a site has no taxonomies at all.
3. **Drop the nav item.** Deletes a row `SPEC/navigation.md` specifies.

Meanwhile the screen keeps the composite title it has always shown — `Categories — Products` — which
names the thing actually on screen. That is an adaptation logged in `BUILD-SPEC.md` §5.9 and it is
explicitly provisional, pending this request.

## What we need

1. **The selection state.** What does `taxonomies.php` render when it is opened with no taxonomy
   chosen — which is what its own nav item does? Its layout, its heading, its empty state when the
   site has no taxonomies at all, and its accessibility (heading level, landmark, focus order).
2. **The heading.** With a taxonomy in context, is the H1 still the flat "Taxonomies" of §32, or
   does it name the taxonomy and its post type? If the latter, its exact composition — the screen
   currently shows `{taxonomy} — {post type}` and that is a build-side holding pattern, not a
   design.
3. **How a person gets from the selection state to a taxonomy**, and whether that is a list, a pair
   of selects, or the same collection entry 19 already renders. If it is the last of those, say so
   and we will reuse it rather than draw a second one.

Nothing else about entry 32 is in question. The add-term form is built to
`template-record-form.md` and needs no answer here.
