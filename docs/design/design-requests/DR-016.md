# DR-016 — What does entry 21's "usage count" count?

Status: **drafted** — 2026-08-30 (D-120). Not sent. The ready-to-paste prompt is
the last section.

**Blocks nothing.** The screen is built and shipping; it counts the thing the
product directly stores and **says on the tile which thing that is**.

## The finding

`SPEC/manifest.md` §21 gives the Blocks tile "wireframe preview 120px · block
name · category · **usage count**", and does not say what is being counted.

Unlike entry 13's due dates or entry 18's settlement lag, this is **not** a
number nobody measured — the product holds the relationship. It just holds it at
one remove, and the two readings are different facts:

| Reading | Where it comes from | What it tells someone |
|---|---|---|
| **templates** | `PageTemplateManager`'s `structure[].block_id` — a template lists the blocks it renders | how many recipes include this block |
| **pages** | every page whose template includes it | how many public URLs actually render it |

A block in **one** template can render on **fifty** pages. On a tile that draws a
bare number beside a name, those two answers look identical and mean opposite
things to someone deciding whether a block is safe to change.

## What the build did, and why

**Templates**, with the label naming them: *"In 2 template(s)"*, *"In no
template"* — never a bare "2" or a bare "Usages".

Three reasons, in order:

1. It is the relationship the product **stores directly**. Pages would be a
   second derivation on top of it, and a derived number is the one that goes
   stale first.
2. It is one traversal for the whole screen rather than one per tile.
3. A label that names its unit cannot be misread. A bare count can, and this is
   precisely the shape where the misreading is expensive.

Counted per TEMPLATE, not per placement: a template that places a block twice
counts once, because the question is "how many templates use this".

## What Design needs to decide

1. **Which does §21 mean** — templates, pages, or both (e.g. "In 2 templates ·
   on 14 pages")?
2. **If pages:** should a block used by a template that no page uses read `0`, or
   should the tile show both figures so the difference is visible?
3. **Is the labelled form right**, or does §21 intend a bare number in the meta
   row beside the category, matching entry 4's tile more closely?
